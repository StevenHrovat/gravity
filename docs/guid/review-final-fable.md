## C. Claim 1

Holds, and is as close to definitional as the remark says. The chain is: Axiom 3 says every witness of the act can read the record in full and any other reader sees the counts; a state readable in full by two parties is broadcastable; broadcastable families commute (Barnum et al. 1996); hence no superposition of two values relative to the witnesses. I looked for a structure DMP defines between parts that escapes: the layout ("recomputed by both halves") is a function of the counts; the trust field's standings are records (Axiom 2); the order of acts is the record's order. None escapes while a record exists. The only escape is erasure (C1). The objection "assumes its conclusion" is fair in the sense the paper already grants: the physics content of Claim 1 is nil; it says what "absolute Observability" costs.

## D. Claim 2 and the debate

Claim 2 states Galley, Giacomini and Selby correctly: their theorem is that three conditions are jointly impossible (gravity generates entanglement; gravity mediates the interaction, in the later version locally; gravity is classical), in generalised probabilistic theories; the paper's contrapositive matches, and "not classical" rather than "quantum" matches the authors' own caution that non-classical does not imply quantum (arXiv:2012.01441, abstract and v6 wording). The one-paragraph treatment of the 2025 debate is accurate: Aziz and Howl, *Nature* 646, 813–817 (2025), DOI 10.1038/s41586-025-09595-7, do argue that classical gravity coupled to quantum matter fields generates entanglement through matter processes; Marletto, Oppenheim, Vedral and Wilson (arXiv:2511.07348, 10 Nov 2025) and Diósi (arXiv:2511.00852, 2 Nov 2025, v2 4 Nov) dispute it as stated. The consequence drawn (only the experimental refuter weakens; Claim 1 is untouched) is right, because Claim 1 never uses Claim 2. Sufficient for the paper; one addition (D2).

## E. Proposition 2 and 2+1

Hosting as defined is nearly empty, and the paper says so ("cheap", "not because it is deep"): G0 leaves content unconstrained, so any content, gravity included, is "hosted" the moment a record stays blind to it. What the evidence actually shows is the classical-control, quantum-payload split of any fault-tolerant quantum computer (syndromes read by a classical controller, logical content untouched), with the controller doubled. That is a true and modest statement; Proposition 2's wording (E1, E2) and the preface's "Showing provability" (P3 below) are not modest. The 2+1 verdict (E4) is the one place where I think the paper is wrong rather than overstated: the superselection rule it keeps is the whole quantum theory in 2+1.

## F. The dissent
