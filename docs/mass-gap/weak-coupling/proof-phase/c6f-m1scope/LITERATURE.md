# Literature scope for M1 (law-level large-field control, 4D lattice SU(2)), cycle 6, c6f-m1scope, 2026-11-22

Question: does any published work after Balaban's 1987-89 series give a law-level (probability measure) or
expectation-value version of his large-field control for 4D lattice gauge theory? M1 = a law-preserving R operation;
M2 = contour-removal lemma giving p ~ exp(-c p0(g)) per cube (definitions: `../c5d-supplier/BALABAN-READING.md`,
section 5).

Method and limits: web search plus abstract and catalogue pages (arXiv abstract pages, publisher and preprint-server
pages). **No full text was read in this session.** Several sites refused automated access (a preprint server's
certificate, the publisher, a scanned-paper host), so for the Magnen-Rivasseau-Seneor paper the content below rests on
a search-engine abstract and a one-sentence statement from a related 1992 preprint seen in a search snippet.
"Review-only" means abstract level. The negative verdict is therefore "no evidence of M1 or M2 found", not "proved
absent from the full texts". Volume and page numbers marked (memory) were not confirmed online.

## By clause

* **M1 (law-preserving R):** found nowhere. Balaban named expectation values as future work (per the c5d reading) and
  no later gauge-theory paper by him turned up. The only later 4D construction with a Schwinger-function claim (item 6)
  is gauge-fixed and infrared cut off.
* **M2 (contour removal, per-cube probability):** found nowhere. Balaban's own estimates (items 1-3) stay the nearest
  ingredient, as the c5d reading says.
* **Related but off the path:** scalar expectation-value RG (item 14) and Dimock's expositions (items 7-9, 11) show the
  shape of the argument in simpler models. None has a non-abelian self-interacting gauge field in d=4.

## Verdict

M1 does not shrink to a citation, not even partly. The literature I could reach contains no law-level or
expectation-value version of Balaban's large-field control for four-dimensional lattice gauge theory. Balaban's series
stops at the integral-preserving R operation, with the coupling theorem's proof unpublished. The one later 4D
construction (Magnen-Rivasseau-Seneor) is a gauge-fixed, infrared-cut-off Schwinger-function construction that gives no
law for the Wilson lattice field. Dimock's work, the scalar RG methods and the recent free-energy and weak-coupling
results concern other models, other dimensions, or leading order only. The only help is methodological: scalar
expectation-value RG suggests how to carry observables through the flow, which may guide redoing the R estimates under a
block constraint, but that work stays paper-scale as scoped in c5d section 5. Confidence limit: abstracts and catalogue
data only. The Magnen-Rivasseau-Seneor paper and the Federbush series are the least checked, and a full-text pass on
those two is the one place a surprise could still come from.
