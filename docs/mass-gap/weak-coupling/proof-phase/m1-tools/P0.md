Brief: Lemma P0 of `D-PIN.md` (an admissible block average, equal to Balaban's on small fields, analytic near pinned
cores, with no trapping), approached as the owner proposes (`../../PREP.md`, last section, "Inside and outside are one
system"): do not treat the pinned core and its surroundings as two regimes; represent the core by the Z2 twist it
imposes on its surroundings and average the surroundings in that twisted sector, where the logarithm's cut should
disappear. Tasks: make it precise, test it with the star harness (trivial shell, then random small-field shells), check

## 0. Result in plain words

1. **The owner's picture becomes one concrete rule.** A pinned core is given a Z2 label (`±1` on each bond of its
   star) once, when it is created. From then on every loop through the core is corrected by its label before the
   logarithm is taken, and the coarse bond gets its label back afterwards. The corrected loops are the loops of a
   smooth field, so **the logarithm's cut never enters**. The label is carried unchanged (it is the core); only the
   corrected, smooth part is averaged.
2. **On small fields it is exactly Balaban's average**: a label can only be created when some loop through the site has
   negative trace (angle above `π/2` from the identity), which never happens on small fields (by construction; confirmed on 2000
   small-field stars).
3. **No trapping in any test.** Random deep stars all become moderate within 6 steps, exactly as with Balaban's own
   average. Stars that start near the vortex are labelled and stay smooth labelled cores. This held with no shell
   noise and with random small-field shells of size 0.05 and 0.15, for three creation thresholds (0.3, 0.6, 1.0). Not
   a single star ended trapped.
4. **Why it works, on paper.** In 4 dimensions the outside of a small core is simply connected, so the twist is pure
   gauge outside the core: the corrected loops are exactly the loops of the outside field after a Z2 gauge change.
   The twisted rule is Balaban's average applied to a smooth field, with the core's label riding along. This is the
   precise form of "the inside asserts itself by not being the outside": the core shows up only in the transports that
   start inside it.
5. **Exactness still holds.** The label is part of the state carried from scale to scale. The step conditions on the
   next field **and** the next labels; the Lean theorem `map_resample_eq` applies verbatim with that larger
   conditioning variable.
6. **Verdict on P0: met in the reduced model; full-lattice write-up still to do.** The twisted rule is admissible, equals
   (0.4) on small fields, is analytic on the twisted sector, and showed no trapping. What remains is routine-looking
   but not done: the definition on the full lattice (labels at coarse sites, symmetric creation rule, locality,
   link reflection), and a proof of no trapping (so far numerical only, in a reduced model).
7. **Effect on P2: it shrinks to a standard estimate.** With the twist pure gauge outside, the outside's renormalisation
   is Balaban's small-field renormalisation of a smooth field, and the label is invisible to the fluctuations (they
   live in the adjoint). What is left of the core's influence is a frozen hole of size `L^{-m}` after `m` steps: in
   `d = 4` a Dirichlet hole changes a Gaussian field by about its capacity, `L^{-2m}`. P2 becomes P2', "subscale hole
   decoupling", open but of a standard kind.

## 1. The twisted rule, precisely (star at a coarse site `y`)

Notation of `NOTE.md` 2.2: star elements `g_b` (the `2d` bonds at `y`), paths `x` with first bond `b(x)`, path
transports `P_x = g_{b(x)} R_x` (`R_x` the rest of the path, in the surroundings), reference `U(c) = g_c R_c`.

* **Labels.** A star is *labelled* by `σ = (σ_b) ∈ {±1}^{2d}`, not all equal, defined up to a global sign. Corrected
  star `h_b = σ_b g_b`.
* **Creation (the root).** An unlabelled deep star (spread `≥ r0`) is labelled when, for some reference bond `r`,
  `σ_b = sign Re tr(g_b g_r^{-1})` is not constant and the corrected star has spread `< θ_c` (`θ_c < π/2`). This is
  the root event of `D-PIN.md` 3.
* **Averaging.** Unlabelled: Balaban's (0.4). Labelled: `Ū(c) = σ_c · exp(∑_x w_x log(h_{b(x)} R_x (h_c R_c)^{-1})) h_c R_c`,
  i.e. (0.4) applied to the corrected star, with the label of `c` put back. The coarse star inherits the label
  `σ` (the core persists, as `vortex_persists` says it must).
* **Drop.** If the corrected spread exceeds `π/2` the label is dropped (not reached in any test).

**Properties (paper).**
* *Small fields:* if every relative element `g_b g_r^{-1}` has `Re tr > 0` (angle `< π/2`), the sign pattern is constant
  for every reference, so no label is created and the rule **is** (0.4).
* *Analytic on the twisted sector:* for a labelled star the logarithm is applied only to corrected loops, which stay
  within `θ_c + O(ε)` of 1; the cut at `-1` is never approached.
* *Well defined and covariant:* changing `σ` to `-σ` changes `h` to `-h`; (0.4) commutes with central factors, so
  `Ū(c)` is unchanged. A gauge transformation at `y` multiplies all `g_b` and `Ū(c)` on the left; labels are defined by
  conjugation-invariant signs; lattice rotations and reflections permute the labels with the bonds.
* *At the exact vortex* (`h` trivial, `σ = s`): `Ū(c) = s_c`, the same output as (0.4) and as `vortex_persists`
  requires; nearby, the rule is analytic, whereas (0.4) jumps (`D-PIN.md` 2.3).
* *Discontinuities:* only at the creation and drop thresholds. Creation is the root event, charged once (`D-PIN.md` 3);
  near the threshold the configuration is treated as a large field with a sharp indicator, exactly as Balaban's
  partitions of unity do ([III] (1.1), (3.2)).

## 2. Why the cut disappears: the twist is pure gauge outside the core (paper)

Take the core `C` to be the unit neighbourhood of `y` (all plaquettes with a corner at `y`). Outside `C` every
plaquette is regular. The complement of a small ball in the 4-dimensional lattice is simply connected, so a regular
field outside `C` is a small field after a gauge transformation `w` on the sites outside `C`, and the Z2 part of `w`
on the boundary of `C` is exactly the label `σ`. Hence the corrected loops `h_{b(x)} R_x (h_c R_c)^{-1}` are the loops of
the `w`-transformed outside field, and the twisted rule is (0.4) on a smooth field followed by putting the label back.
The vortex example makes this concrete: `U = -1` on four bonds at `y` is, outside the 16 plaquettes at `y`, a pure Z2
gauge transformation (`D-PIN.md` era computation, `NOTE.md` 2.4). In the owner's words: the inside is visible only as
the twist in the transports that leave it.

## 3. Numerical checks (`twist_dynamics.py`; `L = 13`, `d = 4`)

Reduced model of `star_dynamics.py`: one star at a block centre; shells modelled by an independent factor
`exp(ε · gaussian)` on every path (4 paths per weight group, so 32 paths per coarse bond). "Trapped" = neither moderate
(spread `< 0.5`) nor a labelled core with corrected spread `< 0.5`.

| run | stars | result after 15 steps |
|---|---|---|
| small fields, any `ε`, `θ_c` | 2000 stars with spread `≤ 0.73` | labels created on 0 stars: the rule is (0.4) there |
| `ε = 0`, `θ_c = 0.6`, 1 path/group, 10000 stars | Haar-random | (0.4): moderate 0.58 / 0.95 / 1.00 after 2 / 3 / 6 steps; twisted rule: the same (0.59 / 0.95 / 1.00), nothing labelled, 0 trapped |
| same | within 0.05 of the vortex | (0.4): expelled, moderate after 6 steps; twisted: all labelled at step 1, all labelled and smooth to step 15, 0 trapped |
| `ε = 0.05` and `ε = 0.15`, `θ_c = 0.6`, 5000 stars | both families | as above: Haar stars all moderate by step 10, near-vortex stars all labelled and smooth; 0 trapped |
| `ε = 0.05`, `θ_c = 0.3` | both | same; 0 trapped |
| `ε = 0.05`, `θ_c = 1.0` | both | Haar: 0.08% labelled (and smooth), the rest moderate; 0 trapped |

Limits of the model: one site only (neighbouring stars and the real block geometry are not simulated); shell noise is
independent per path, whereas real shells are correlated; the drop rule was never triggered, so its behaviour is
untested; the creation threshold is crossed only at the first steps in these runs.

## 4. Exactness with a frozen core and labels (paper; Lean theorem reused)

The state at scale `m` becomes `(V_m, Σ_m)`: the block field and the finite set of carried labels (each a pattern in
`{±1}^{2d}` mod sign at a coarse site, plus the frozen root data of `D-PIN.md` 1). The twisted blocking is a
deterministic measurable map `(V_m, Σ_m) ↦ (V_{m+1}, Σ_{m+1})` (labels are signs of polynomials in the group entries:
measurable). The labels are functions of the fine field, so the twisted block field at every scale is a measurable
function of the bare field, and its law is a pushforward.

`map_resample_eq` (`formal/ProofPhase/M1/Exact.lean`) holds for any measurable `φ : α → γ`. Take `α` = the current
configuration together with its labels, and `φ = (W, Σ_{m+1}, V_out, frozen root data)`. The next twisted blocking
factors through `φ`, so `map_comp_resample_eq` gives: the conditional step preserves the joint law of the next field and
the next labels, hence the law of the twisted block field at the scale where it is used. No new Lean is needed: the
theorem was stated for an arbitrary measurable conditioning variable.

What changes downstream: the field at `L_*` is the block field of the **twisted** blocking. It is identical to
Balaban's on small fields; locality (labels at `y` depend only on fields near `y`), gauge covariance and the lattice
symmetries hold by construction (section 1); link reflection positivity needs the creation rule to commute with link
reflections, which a symmetric rule does (to check when written out).

**P2 becomes P2' (subscale hole decoupling).** Because the twist is pure gauge outside the core (section 2) and centre
labels act trivially in the adjoint, the outside is renormalised exactly as a smooth small field. What remains of the
core's influence after `m` steps is the frozen hole, of size `L^{-m}` in current units: P2' asks that its effect on the
conditional step's ratio `N/D` be `exp(O(C L^{-c m}))` (capacity heuristic: `c = 2` in `d = 4`). This is a standard
type of estimate (Gaussian field with a small Dirichlet hole, plus Balaban's localization); open, about 2-3 pages. P3
stays the fallback if P2' fails.
