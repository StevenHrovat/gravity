# Pre-registration (design only, NOT run): is the conditional fine field massive in time at L1 sqrt(sigma) = 2.80? (c4c-seg2, 2026-11-22)

Status: **written, not run.** Needs new code (a conditional sampler, below), a CPU pipeline test, and the coordinator's decision to
queue it. No GPU was touched by this workstream. Purpose: test the named hypothesis `SmootherStable` (`../../../formal/ProofPhase/
Seg2/Filter.lean`; note section 3) for the good blocking at the counted point of c3b, i.e. measure the quantity of part (c) of
`ObsTransferPhys` in the time direction directly, at order-one coupling, with positive and negative controls.

## Question

Given the L1 block field of the good blocking (w = 2, as in `../c3b-refit/PREREG.md`), do time-separated local observables of
the fine field decorrelate at a rate of order 1/L1 (observability carries the conditional mass), or does the conditional
correlation track the unconditional one (block field near noise: Lemma X = the gap)?

## The conditional law (soft blocking)

Exact conditioning on the deterministic block field is impractical (a nonlinear constraint per coarse link). Use the **soft
blocking** (the noisy form recommended in c3a section 8 and c3d section 5): joint law of fine field U and block variable Y

  `P(U, Y) = mu_W(U) * prod_l p_kappa(Y_l ; Phi_l(U))`,  `p_kappa(Y; V) ∝ exp(kappa * (1/2) Re tr(Y^dag V))` (Haar in Y),

with `Phi` the good blocking (w = 2, `goodblock.py`, unchanged). Sampling Y given an equilibrium U0 (Y_l = Phi_l(U0) * eta_l, eta_l
drawn from `exp(kappa (1/2) Re tr eta)`) makes **U0 an exact draw from the conditional law given Y**: no thermalisation bias for
replica a. Replica b is generated by HMC on the conditional action `S_W(U) - kappa sum_l (1/2) Re tr(Y_l^dag Phi_l(U))`, started
from an independent equilibrium configuration U1; forces by automatic differentiation through the smearing and the SU(2)
projection `A -> A / sqrt(det A)` (analytic off det A = 0, a null set). The thermalisation of replica b is itself a measurement
(conditional mixing time; reported).

## Observable and estimator

* F at coarse time slab tau: the spatial-plaquette trace (1/2 Re tr) averaged over the fine hypercube of side b/2 **centred between
  coarse time slices** (c3a's "centre" placement), at a fixed coarse spatial cell; G the same at tau + D, same cell. D = 0, 1, 2, 3
  (coarse steps). All cells and time origins averaged.
* Conditional correlation `Gamma(D) = mean over Y and positions of [F^a G^a - F^a G^b]` (estimates `E_Y Cov(F, G | Y)`, the
  `condDefect` of part (c), with the soft blocking).
* Unconditional `C(D)` from the unconditioned configurations. Ratios `R(D) = Gamma(D)/C(D)`, profile `Gamma(D)/Gamma(0)`; fitted
  conditional rate `m_c L1` from D = 1, 2 (and 3 if resolved at 2 sigma).
* Content check (c3b's rule) on the same configurations, so the point counts only if the block variable has content.

## Gates and verdicts (fixed now)

* **Photon gate (run 1):** at kappa = 32, `Gamma(2)/Gamma(0) <= 0.05` and `R(2) <= 0.3` at both b, the fitted rates within a factor
  1.5 of each other (resolution-uniform), and `C(D)` not exponentially small (power law). If it fails, stop: the sampler or the
  estimator is wrong (c3a's Gaussian computation predicts a rate of about 4.5/L1).
* **Decimation gate (run 2):** `R(2)` at w = 0 exceeds the w = 2 value by at least 0.2. If not, the test cannot distinguish an
  informative from an uninformative block variable: report INCONCLUSIVE for SU(2).
* **SU(2) (runs 3, 4), kappa = 32:**
  * **MASSIVE-CONDITIONAL:** both points `m_c L1 >= 2` and `R(2) <= 0.3`, rates within 30 % of each other, and the kappa trend
    not falling from 8 to 32 by more than 30 %. Reading: observability carries the conditional mass at the counted point;
    `SmootherStable` is supported at one size; the gap content sits in `LevelDprime` (W1 form), which c3b found reached.
  * **NOISE-LIKE:** `R(2) >= 0.7` at either point. Reading: the block variable is near noise in the time channel; Lemma X carries
    the gap there; the route must move L1 or the blocking.
  * **SLOW-CONDITIONAL:** `0.3 < R(2) < 0.7` or `m_c L1 < 2`: a conditional correlation length of order L1 or more; a candidate
    hidden (conditional) near-transition; report the replica-b mixing times alongside.
  * otherwise INCONCLUSIVE.

## Expectations (not criteria)

MASSIVE-CONDITIONAL at kappa = 8 and 32, with `m_c L1` lower than the photon's 4.5 (interacting, order-one coupling), and a longer
replica-b thermalisation for SU(2) than for U(1).

## What this cannot show

One physical box and one L1; annealed (Y-averaged) only, the form the Lean hypothesis uses; soft blocking only (the kappa trend is
the evidence for the hard limit); volume uniformity is not tested (fixed box); and the result is a statement about conditional
decorrelation, which by itself carries no gap (the photon passes it).

## Follow-on (design sketch, not pre-registered): a cell-local conditional Dobrushin-Shlosman test

The dimension-free sufficient condition (note section 4.3) is local: for one L1 cell with fine boundary links fixed and its block
variables given (soft), the influence of changing the boundary on one face on the law of block-scale observables near the opposite
face, summed over faces, below 1 (Dobrushin-Shlosman finite-volume form; W1 metric as in c3c). Finite, local and certifiable in
principle; it is the numerical form of c4a's `CondMass`.
