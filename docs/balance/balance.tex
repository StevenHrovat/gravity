\section{Foundations}\label{sec:found}

\begin{definition}[the two fields]\label{def:fields}
The \emph{existence field} is a frame of four vector fields $e_a{}^\mu$, one for each direction that spans spacetime, three of space and one
of time; the metric it carries is $g_{\mu\nu}=\eta_{ab}e^a{}_\mu e^b{}_\nu$. The \emph{information field} is a scalar $\psi$ whose value
sets the fine-structure constant, $\alpha=\alpha(\psi)$. DMP's trust half is the existence field; its information half is the information
field and the matter it acts on.
\end{definition}

\noindent This is the structure the first author has meant from the start: ``one existence, one information'', a vector field that ``can
span a relativistic plane'' and a scalar field that ``can span an information plane'' (29 October, 15:25Z). In physics the frame is the
tetrad, with which general relativity can be written~\cite{aldrovandi}, and a scalar that sets $\alpha$ is Bekenstein's theory of a varying
fine-structure constant~\cite{bekenstein82}.

\begin{lemma}[the existence field is classical]\label{lem:classical}
Every value of the existence field can be held in full by every witness at once, so its possible states commute.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Axiom~3 \tagD: every grant, use and loss of trust leaves a record ``that every witness of that act can read in full''. A record two
witnesses each hold in full is a copy for each. One physical process that copies two states $|s\rangle,|t\rangle$ preserves their inner
product, so $\langle s|t\rangle=\langle s|t\rangle^2\in\{0,1\}$ (Wootters and Zurek~\cite{wz}); for mixed states a set can be broadcast
exactly when it commutes (Barnum et al.~\cite{barnum}) \tagP. The values of the trust half's field are such records, so they commute.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[cannot model, can host]\label{lem:host}
DMP's trust half cannot be a field that entangles; DMP's information half can carry any quantum content.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
A classical mediator cannot entangle two systems it acts on locally (Galley, Giacomini and Selby~\cite{ggs}) \tagP, which with
Lemma~\ref{lem:classical} gives the first part. Axiom~3 restricts what is \emph{derived from trust}, not what the information half holds:
content moves by a grant and need not be broadcast, so nothing forbids non-commuting states there \tagD.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{The spin ladder.} The first author's reading of spin, from the same afternoon (16:21Z, read with two scalars at spin~0 at
16:23Z), sharpened at 21:46Z: ``Spin zero is existence itself, so only one part. Spin one is information itself, which when delivered is an

right-handed halves; spin~1 the force carriers; spin~2 the wave of the existence frame, which a theorem of Weinberg and Deser makes general
relativity~\cite{weinberg64,deser70}. The notes below build on this ladder.

\section{A spinning hole drags existence along}\label{sec:kerr}

The first author, 18:57Z: ``Is the information a spinning bh leaks just it dragging Existence along? That would make axiomatically way more
sense, since no act, by definition, should be able to cross the horizon, only existences switching G0 chain h by h. That also nicely lines
up with current observations and understanding, to my knowledge, so it looks good.''

\paragraph{Test.} A spinning black hole drags spacetime around with it (frame dragging) \tagP. Inside the \emph{ergosphere}, which reaches
$r=2GM/c^2$ at the equator for every spin and lies outside the horizon $r_+=(GM/c^2)(1+\sqrt{1-a^2})$ \chk{B4}, nothing can stand still
against that drag. Every known way to take energy from a spinning hole works there, outside the horizon: Penrose's process with
particles~\cite{penrose69}, superradiance with waves, and the Blandford--Znajek process with magnetic fields~\cite{bz}, the leading model of
the jets of active galaxies. What can be taken is the rotational energy, $1-M_{\text{irr}}/M$, up to 29\% at maximal spin, while the horizon
area never decreases (Christodoulou~\cite{christodoulou}; \chk{B4}). Nothing crosses the horizon outwards. So ``dragging Existence along''
and ``no act should be able to cross the horizon'' are exact: what leaves is energy of the frame's rotation, extracted outside. In the
language of Section~\ref{sec:found}, the leak is a property of the classical existence frame, not an act of the information half.
\emph{Verdict: holds.} One correction of words: what leaks is energy and angular momentum, not the information about what fell in.

\section{Inflation as the possibility space}\label{sec:inflation}

The first author, 17:14Z: ``How I see inflation: Inflation is just the Possibility space of Existence. If there COULD be Existence there, it
will have the space to do so. This logically also explains the extremely flat curvature of spacetime, if one assumes the perfect duality as
G0. At that point the only possibility was "anywhere but here", so inflation was at it's maximum, form there, as more possibilities to Exist
appeared, there was less of a need for there to be more. Currently it's at "meh, it's 'aight" and will eventually achieve equilibrium. That
means the big chill, which is the best outcome, is likely the one coming, which seems appropriate for all the chill vibes :D''

\paragraph{Flatness.} Inflation was proposed to explain exactly this: a curvature of order one is diluted by $e^{-2N}$ over $N$ e-folds of
exponential expansion (Guth~\cite{guth}) \tagP. After inflation the curvature share $|\Omega-1|$ grows again, as $a^2$ while radiation
dominates and as $a$ while matter does; from a reheating temperature of $10^{15}$\,GeV to today it grows by about $5\times10^{51}$
\chk{B1}. Planck finds $|\Omega_k|$ below a few parts per thousand~\cite{planck18}, so an initial curvature of order one needs about 62
e-folds \chk{B1}, the textbook ``sixty''. ``Inflation was at it's maximum'' at the start, ``anywhere but here'', is the first author's
picture of that dilution \tagR. \emph{Verdict: holds as a reading} of inflation's solution to the flatness problem; it adds the motive (the
possibility to exist), not a number.

\paragraph{``Less of a need'', and equilibrium.} In the flat $\Lambda$CDM model that fits the data, the Hubble rate $H$ falls at every
moment, from the past into the far future, and tends to a constant, the de Sitter rate $H_\infty=\sqrt{\Omega_\Lambda}\,H_0\approx0.83\,H_0$
\chk{B2}. A universe that settles into de Sitter expansion cools towards a final, empty state: the ``Big Chill'' or ``Big Freeze'' is the
standard prediction of $\Lambda$CDM \tagP. So ``less of a need for there to be more'' and ``will eventually achieve equilibrium'' hold, read
as the falling Hubble rate and its de Sitter floor. Two remarks. The expansion has \emph{accelerated} again since redshift $z\approx0.63$
\chk{B2}, when dark energy began to dominate; that is a second phase, distinct from the early inflation, which the reading does not yet
name. And the newest data leave the end open: DESI's second data release prefers a dark energy that weakens with time at 2.8 to 4.2
standard deviations, depending on the supernova sample~\cite{desi2}; if that holds, the future is not exactly de Sitter. \emph{Verdict:
holds as a reading} in $\Lambda$CDM.

\section{The Higgs boson as pure existence}\label{sec:higgs}

The first author's notes of 20:41Z and 21:27Z asked whether the Higgs field must exist at all (``Must the Higgs field even exist in my
system? [\dots] I must discard it until it's need becomes pressing'', ``Yeah, Higgs field seems very unlikely, since it quantizes
Existence''). After we showed him the data he refined it, 21:46Z: ``The Higgs boson would theoretically be all existence, and thus be unable
to observe itself. So it could not exist in isolation. I agree that the particles that get existence do get existence and do have mass. But
I instead posit that they get that through their inherent causality link to G0, and thus simply from the existence field, so the
gravitational field itself, because it is an information from G0, and thus it must exist. So the existential field asserts the existence of
the information.''

\paragraph{``All existence, unable to exist in isolation''.} This fits two measured facts closely. The Higgs boson does not persist: in the
Standard Model it decays in $\hbar/\Gamma\approx1.6\times10^{-22}$\,s \chk{B13}, and it is seen only through its decay products, which peak
at 125\,GeV~\cite{atlas12,cms12}. And it couples to every particle in proportion to that particle's mass, which in the first author's terms
is its existence: the LHC has measured this for the W, the Z, the top and bottom quarks, the tau and the muon~\cite{atlas22,cms22}. A field
that is ``all existence'' and talks to each thing in the measure of its existence is a fair picture of both. \emph{Verdict: holds as a
reading.}

\paragraph{``They get it from the gravitational field itself''.} This part does not survive. Gravity is far too weak to be what sets a
particle's mass: its coupling to the electron, $Gm_e^2/\hbar c=1.8\times10^{-45}$, is more than $10^{39}$ times smaller than the Higgs
coupling $y_e=\sqrt2\,m_e/v=2.9\times10^{-6}$ that does \chk{B13}. A mass-giving field must also keep the scattering of W bosons below
certainty: without the Higgs, its probability would exceed one above about 1\,TeV (Lee, Quigg and Thacker~\cite{lqt}; \chk{B8}), an energy
the LHC exceeds, and gravity does nothing there. \emph{Verdict: refuted} as ``masses come from the gravitational field instead of the
Higgs field''; and ``the Higgs field does not exist'' is refuted by the boson's observation.

\paragraph{A reading that keeps both.} The first author's structure has room for the Higgs field exactly where he puts existence at spin~0:
the Higgs field \emph{is} the spin-0 existence that particles carry, and the gravitational field, the existence frame, responds to it, since
gravity couples to mass. This is our proposal \tagR, not his words, and it is the Standard Model read in his terms. It also answers his
worry that the Higgs field ``quantizes Existence'': the Higgs field is matter, content of the information half, while the existence frame,
which Lemma~\ref{lem:classical} keeps classical, is gravity. A quantum Higgs field and a classical frame live on different halves. DMP's
axioms need no Higgs field, and by Lemma~\ref{lem:host} they can host one.

\section{The forces as tiers of one IM}\label{sec:forces}

The first author, 20:41Z, first grouped the forces as ``IM=strong+weak'' and ``GG=EM''; electroweak unification groups them the other way
(the photon and the Z are mixtures of the same pair of fields~\cite{glashow,weinberg,salam}, and \chk{B9} recovers their mixing angle,
$\sin^2\theta_W=0.2304$, from three independent measurements). He replaced the grouping at 21:46Z: ``Since in the end it's all one field,
because I think I mentioned that somewhere, unified field. In the end it's all information and existence. And what fields information uses to
assert existence [\dots] is irrelevant. That's why once causality was established, the forces separated, because the current configuration
was strong, binding it together, weak, keeping the cluster stable, and EM keeping the cluster of clusters stable. So yeah, it's one. Yeah,
it's all IM, but it's different tiers of IM.''

\paragraph{The tiers.} Each force does the job he names \tagP. The strong force binds quarks into protons and neutrons and those into nuclei.
The weak force turns neutrons into protons and back, and so moves every nucleus towards the stable ratio of neutrons to protons, the valley of
stability; it is the force that keeps a nucleus from staying in an unstable configuration. Electromagnetism binds nuclei and electrons into
atoms, and atoms into molecules: the cluster of clusters. \emph{Verdict: holds as a reading.}

\paragraph{``All one field''.} That the forces separate as the universe cools is the standard picture: the electroweak symmetry breaks at
about 246\,GeV, and grand unified theories put the strong force with the other two at a still higher energy \tagP. Whether they truly join
is open. Run with the measured couplings, the three inverse strengths of the Standard Model come within about 10\% of each other near
$10^{14}$\,GeV, but they do not meet at one point \chk{B14}; meeting needs new particles or new physics above the reach of today's colliders.
\emph{Verdict: untested}, a live hypothesis.

\paragraph{Gluons bounce forever.} At 21:52Z: ``since they don't actually have any existence, they just information, and just have mass
through the information, through quantization, they can interact and, you know, bounce around, stability, good vibes, for as far as I
assume, pretty much forever. [\dots] So are they just constantly bouncing around between quarks? Do they stop after one?'' They never stop.
Inside a proton gluons are emitted, absorbed and split into gluons and quark pairs continuously; one light crossing of the proton takes
$2.8\times10^{-24}$\,s \chk{B12}, about $10^{23}$ crossings per second, for as long as the proton exists, which is more than $10^{34}$
years~\cite{pdg}. Gluons have no rest mass, but their energy is real: it is most of the proton's mass~\cite{yang}, and in a fast proton the
gluons carry about half of the momentum~\cite{pdg}. \emph{Verdict: holds.}

\paragraph{``137 times''.} At 21:46Z: ``I would assume that the existence derived from the internal affirmation or assertion of it is 130
times higher than the assertion from information. That's also my explanation for the bandgap. Because 137th is the share that must be paid
for information to exist. So the part that existence can't balance itself.'' Read as a ratio of strengths, this holds at the right place: the
strong coupling grows towards low energy and reaches 1 near 0.2 to 1\,GeV, the scale of the proton, where it is then about 137 times $\alpha$
\chk{B9}; at the Z mass the ratio is only 15. Read as a share of mass, it does not: the electromagnetic self-energy of the proton is about
1\,MeV of its 938, one part in about 900, not one in 137 \chk{B15}. \emph{Verdict: holds as a ratio of couplings at the hadron scale; refuted
as a share of mass.} ``The share that must be paid for information to exist'' is the first author's reading of $\alpha$ \tagR\ and gives no
further number: \emph{untested}.

\section{Quantum computation, briefly}\label{sec:qc}

The first author, 17:57Z: ``QC is not a subset of DMP.QG, as that would not be possible, instead it is ANY subset of DMP.'' And at 21:52Z:

\paragraph{Test.} In quantum electrodynamics $\alpha$ is the strength with which a charge interacts with the electromagnetic field, and so,

interact with itself'' is a faithful reading of what $\alpha$ is. ``Before it needs to reground itself, through Causality, to G0'' is the

changes with it.

\section{Where we expect it to break}\label{sec:break}
\begin{enumerate}[nosep]
\item \emph{The foundations.} Lemma~\ref{lem:classical} rests on reading Axiom~3's record as held in full by every witness, and on
identifying the trust half with the existence frame. A different reading of either removes Lemmas~\ref{lem:classical} and~\ref{lem:host},
and with them the reading of the Higgs field as matter on the information side.
\item \emph{The cosmology.} The verdict on the Big Chill is $\Lambda$CDM's; if the weakening dark energy DESI prefers is confirmed, the far
future changes and the ``equilibrium'' reading must be redone.
\item \emph{Unification.} If the three couplings are shown to meet (with new particles) the ``all one field'' row turns to holds; if
proton decay stays unseen far beyond today's limits, grand unification at $10^{14}$ to $10^{16}$\,GeV loses ground.
\item \emph{The checks.} \chk{B1} uses a reheating temperature of $10^{15}$\,GeV; a lower one needs fewer e-folds, which does not change
the reading. \chk{B9} and \chk{B14} run couplings at one loop; \chk{B15} treats the proton as a uniformly charged sphere. Better inputs move
the numbers by tens of per cent, not the verdicts.
\item \emph{The readings.} Most verdicts are ``holds as a reading'': they agree with known physics and predict nothing new. One of them
becomes physics only when it gives a number standard physics does not.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Conclusion}

The first author's evening notes, with his own corrections, read as a faithful map of known physics onto his two fields. A spinning horizon
gives up only the rotation of the classical frame, from outside. Inflation is the possibility to exist that flattened space, and the Big
Chill its equilibrium. The Higgs boson is pure existence that cannot exist alone, and the field behind it is the spin-0 existence of matter
to which gravity responds; what does not survive is gravity giving mass by itself. The forces are tiers of one interaction, whose final
unity is open. Information is the witness, as decoherence and quantum Darwinism say. And $\alpha$, his BSD, is the strength with which
existence interacts with itself. None of the failures is a failure of DMP's three axioms, because DMP does not model the physics it reads. It
hosts it.
