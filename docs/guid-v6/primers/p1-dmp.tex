\begin{primer}{DMP and trust systems}{box:dmp}
\setlength{\parskip}{3pt plus 1pt}%
\emph{What a trust system is for.} In a computer system an access-control or trust protocol decides, for every request, who may do what
to which information, and it keeps the evidence of what was done. Its classic rules are to grant the least privilege an act needs, to
demand two independent keys for what matters, and to keep a complete audit trail~\cite{saltzer1975}. Its formal tools include lattices
that say where information may flow~\cite{denning}, noninterference, which says what one side may learn of the other~\cite{gm}, and quorums
of witnesses that fail independently~\cite{mr}. DMP~\cite{dmp} states such a system as three axioms and a ground, G0; every later
statement is a numbered theorem (T) or derived constraint (C).

\emph{Words and the gate.} A \emph{part} is ``a Duality of Trust'': a trust half and an information half. An \emph{act} is what a part
does. A \emph{layer} is where trust over acts is decided, and its two \emph{witnesses} are its halves: the \emph{trust half} decides whether
the act may happen, the \emph{information half} whether it is meant and right. Two witnesses are \emph{independent} when they share no
member of the \emph{failure classes} the layer declares (host, key, author, engine, person, time source, and whatever the minting lists).
Each returns a verdict in the lattice $\textsf{refuse}<\textsf{hold}<\textsf{pass}$, and the \emph{gate} is their meet,
$\operatorname{gate}_L(a,t)=v_{w_T}(a,t)\wedge v_{w_I}(a,t)$: the stricter answer stands, and the act is performed only on
$\textsf{pass}\wedge\textsf{pass}$. A \emph{standing} is trust held in the field of Axiom~2, with a source (the witness that granted it), a
depth (its \emph{grant chain} back to the first pair) and a freshness that decays; \emph{staleness} is that decay. A \emph{record} is
evidence that outlives its act.

\emph{The axioms.} \emph{Axiom~1, the Duality:} a part acts only while both halves of its layer trust it for that act, and neither
overrules the other; its \emph{balance} moves every part towards the trust its boundary declares. \emph{Axiom~2, Trust:} a witness trusts
only on evidence it verified itself; ``Trust is gravitational. It is a field, never a position.'' \emph{Axiom~3, Information:} every grant,
use and loss of trust leaves a record that every witness of the act reads in full; any other reader sees the \emph{counts} always and the
content only by a \emph{grant}; from trust, nothing about information can be derived but its existence. Every layer is a tree rooted at
its own Duality, and its \emph{layout}, where an item sits, is ``the median of that demand'', the place of fewest hops in total,
recomputed by both halves and never by one.

\emph{A worked act} (Figure~\ref{fig:act}). A part $p$ asks to change one entry of a directory. The trust half checks $p$'s standing:
granted by a witness, two grants from the minted pair, still fresh: \textsf{pass}. The information half finds the change well formed but
not yet confirmed: \textsf{hold}. The gate gives $\textsf{pass}\wedge\textsf{hold}=\textsf{hold}$, and nothing is done. Once the change is
confirmed, both return \textsf{pass}, the act is performed, and a record is written at the act that both witnesses can read in full. A
reader outside the layer, say an auditor, sees the counts (one more act of this layer) but not the new entry, unless it is granted to that
reader. From the trust side alone one learns only that the entry exists.

\emph{Containment and G0.} A boundary \emph{contains} information when no part beyond it is trusted for any act on that information; a
strong enough boundary ``bends trustspace'' so that only entropy leaves. G0: ``The dual gate cannot witness its own first instance:
somebody has to trust the first two witnesses.'' The first act, the \emph{minting}, makes at least five parts the holders of the
\emph{root keys}; it is stated, not derived, and every later standing descends from it through grant chains.

\emph{The theorems this paper cites.} \emph{T4}: every parameter is configuration; DMP fixes structure, never a value, and derives no
constant. \emph{T27}: a contained item is destroyed at its scheduled date. \emph{T31}: contained information reaches no part by any act;
what still leaves is minimal, thermal emission, the \emph{entropy floor}, which ``carries entropy and no information'', and outside one
learns only the size, the staleness and the rotation interval. \emph{T32}: the VIBE field; each member has a depth, a mass (one plus the
members deriving through it) and a shortfall, and the potential $\Phi_{\mathrm V}\ge0$ never rises under the system's own acts and vanishes
in the \emph{balanced state}; readers other than the witnesses see one number, the boundary's temperature, its mass-weighted staleness.
\emph{T37}: information that no admission names is contained at the item itself. \emph{T40}: a minting originates only from G0 together
with a state in which every axiom and theorem provably holds.
\end{primer}
