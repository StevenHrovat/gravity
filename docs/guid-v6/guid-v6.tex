\begin{abstract}
\noindent GUID, the General Unified Information Duality, reads an information-theoretic axiom system, the Dark Matter Protocol (DMP~0.9.9),
as physics. Every act needs two independent witnesses, one owning trust and one owning information; trust is a field; every act leaves a
record that its witnesses read in full while any other reader sees only counts. Read this way, gravity is a classical frame, the tetrad
of general relativity, shaped by the counts of energy and momentum, over quantum content. From the axioms, with the identifications
stated as hypotheses, we derive universal free fall, $\gamma=1$ for the metric, and, by the no-broadcasting theorem, that the frame is
classical and cannot mediate entanglement. Consistency with quantum mechanics then makes the frame a channel that decoheres matter and
diffuses, which we identify with measurement and feedback; fixed by two bridge hypotheses of its own, its strength lands on the
Di\'osi--Penrose rate, with a nuclear smearing length left to experiment. This paper states the whole account in one place and tests it two ways. \emph{By
simulation:} we host four known, hard quantum configurations and recover their known results, run the holographic assembly at thousands
of qubits, and simulate 4000 gravitating masses under a recorded frame, against a coherent field simulated in full for 24 masses and
pairwise for 4000. \emph{Against data:} the rotation curves of 147 SPARC galaxies refute one of the two bridges and the stochastic
dark-matter remnant in its computed form; its accumulated form fits them only as Milgrom's interpolating function and fails in the solar
system. The dark-matter reading that survives is primordial black holes from the start, consistent with cold-dark-matter halos. Of 33
scored confrontations with laboratory, solar-system, astrophysical and cosmological data, 30 agree at 95\%, among them all 13 that test
GUID-specific statements; the three tensions are inherited from $\Lambda$CDM, and the named inflation model meets a fourth. The simulations also show that, if Di\'osi--Penrose decoherence is real,
no entanglement test can separate classical gravity from quantum gravity with that decoherence; the first experiment to decide is
whether the decoherence exists, which tests Bridge~B. The spin witness of the original proposal, as printed in its arXiv version, is
reached by product states, and the form quoted in later work is valid but detects only large entangling phases; we give a two-setting
witness that detects every entangling phase.
Every number comes from public scripts with 496 passing checks. We claim that the construction exists, reproduces and is consistent with
the data named, not that it is correct.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}\label{sec:intro}

\subsection{The problem}

General relativity describes gravity as the curvature of a classical spacetime metric $g_{\mu\nu}$ sourced by energy and momentum,
$G_{\mu\nu}+\Lambda g_{\mu\nu}=(8\pi G/c^4)T_{\mu\nu}$. Quantum theory describes every other interaction as a quantum field. The two
meet in three places, and in each the meeting is not understood.

\emph{The ultraviolet.} Quantising the metric perturbatively around flat space, $g_{\mu\nu}=\eta_{\mu\nu}+\kappa h_{\mu\nu}$ with
$\kappa=\sqrt{32\pi G}/c^2$, gives a massless spin-2 particle, the graviton, whose coupling has the dimension of a length. The theory is
not renormalisable: pure gravity diverges at two loops (Goroff and Sagnotti~\cite{goroff}), and with matter already at one loop ('t~Hooft and
Veltman~\cite{thooftveltman}). Below the Planck energy $E_P=\sqrt{\hbar c^5/G}\approx1.22\times10^{19}$\,GeV the theory is nevertheless a
reliable effective field theory (Donoghue~\cite{donoghue}); its leading quantum correction to the Newtonian potential is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:eft}
V(r)=-\frac{Gm_1m_2}{r}\left[1+3\frac{G(m_1+m_2)}{rc^2}+\frac{41}{10\pi}\frac{G\hbar}{r^2c^3}\right],
\end{equation}
where the middle term is classical and the last is quantum~\cite{bdh}. At a distance of one metre the quantum term is about
$3\times10^{-70}$ of the first \chk{Q1}. What completes the theory above $E_P$ is the subject of string theory, loop quantum gravity,
asymptotic safety, causal dynamical triangulations, causal sets and others (Section~\ref{sec:landscape}).

\emph{Black holes.} A black hole radiates thermally at the Hawking temperature $k_BT_H=\hbar c^3/8\pi GM$~\cite{hawking75} and carries the
Bekenstein--Hawking entropy $S=k_Bc^3A/4G\hbar$~\cite{bekenstein73}. Whether the radiation carries the information that fell in, and how,
is the black-hole information problem~\cite{page93,penington,aemm}.

\emph{Whether to quantise at all.} Nothing in experiment yet requires the gravitational field itself to be quantum. Feynman's argument for
quantising it, the Eppley--Hannah thought experiment~\cite{eppley} and the Page--Geilker experiment~\cite{pagegeilker} rule out specific
ways of coupling a classical metric to quantum matter, not all of them. A consistent classical-quantum dynamics exists and is testable
(Oppenheim~\cite{oppenheim}; Oppenheim, Sparaciari, \v{S}oda and Weller-Davies~\cite{osswd}), and the question has become experimental:
if gravity can entangle two masses through local interaction, it is not classical (Bose et al.~\cite{bose}; Marletto and Vedral~\cite{mv};
Galley, Giacomini and Selby~\cite{ggs}).

\subsection{The proposal in brief}

GUID, the General Unified Information Duality, is the reading of an information-theoretic axiom system, the Dark Matter Protocol
(DMP~0.9.9~\cite{dmp}), as physics. DMP was written to govern trust in computer systems. Its first axiom asks that every act be passed by
two independent witnesses, one owning trust and one owning information; its second calls trust ``gravitational'', a field with sources,
depths and a freshness that decays; its third asks that every act leave a record that its witnesses read in full while every other reader
sees only counts, and that nothing about information be derivable from trust except that it exists. The structural resemblance to physics
is deliberate: a field that sources from mass, boundaries that contain, a floor at which only entropy leaves.

Taken literally, that resemblance gives one account of gravity and of what it contains (Part~I). Gravity is the trust field: a classical
frame, the tetrad of general relativity, shaped by the counts of energy and momentum but never identical to what it contains. It falls
universally, does not mix with the information half, and is classical once the start of the universe is complete, because the axioms make
every one of its values a record that two witnesses hold in full; therefore it mediates no entanglement. Supplied with the base
configuration currently assumed for our universe, DMP hosts this theory: its shared structure carries the classical frame, and its
information half carries any quantum content. A classical frame coupled to quantum matter must decohere that matter and must itself
diffuse, with a trade-off between the two; the axioms' own architecture, record then recompute, is exactly the measurement-and-feedback
channel that realises it, and two bridge principles fix its one free constant. The same reading covers horizons, black holes, holographic
codes, lower dimensions, the start of the universe, the Higgs vacuum, the fine-structure constant, and the dark matter, which the theory
lets be remnants of interactions trapped in the gravitational field.

\subsection{What this paper does}

Version~5.1 of this work stated the account and computed what follows from it. This version tests it, in two ways, and states the result
in one self-contained paper.

\emph{The theory as simulated} (Part~II). If DMP hosts quantum content, a classical computer can host known, hard quantum
configurations as existence and recover their known results; if the frame is a recorded classical channel, simulating it against a
coherent field must show the difference. We run both.

\emph{The predictions against data} (Part~III). Every prediction of Part~I that existing data can reach is confronted with them, with
stated likelihoods, pulls and verdicts, and with the choices made after seeing the data listed.

The results, in brief:
\begin{enumerate}[nosep]
\item \emph{Hosting works and tests no new physics.} A Heisenberg ring, a transverse-field Ising chain of 4000 sites at criticality, the
lattice Schwinger model and Shor's factoring circuit are hosted and recover their known results: the ground-state energy per site to
$1.5\times10^{-6}$, the central charge $1/2$ to $5\times10^{-9}$, the mass gap $1/\sqrt\pi$ to 7\%, and Shor's outcome distribution to
$10^{-11}$ (Section~\ref{sec:hosted}). The holographic assembly runs at thousands of qubits (Section~\ref{sec:holocode}).
\item \emph{The recorded frame and the coherent field differ as the theory says.} For 4000 gravitating masses the recorded frame keeps
every trajectory a product state while reproducing Newton's force; a coherent field, simulated in full for up to 24 masses and pairwise for
4000, drives small systems towards the Page entropy and Porter--Thomas statistics. A classical-quantum toy reproduces the decoherence-diffusion trade-off, and the Kafri--Taylor--Milburn channel
reaches its minimal heating (Section~\ref{sec:gravsim}).
\item \emph{If Di\'osi--Penrose decoherence is real, no entanglement test decides.} No arrangement of two solid spheres then becomes
entangled even if gravity is quantum, because the ratio of the entangling to the decoherence rate is at most 1 while entanglement needs more
than 2. The first experiment to decide is whether that decoherence exists, which tests Bridge~B; only in its absence does the entanglement
test separate classical from quantum gravity. The spin witness of the original proposal, as printed in its arXiv version, is reached by
product states, most likely a misprint; the form quoted in later work is valid but detects only entangling phases above $\pi/2$, and a
two-setting witness that detects every entangling phase is given (Section~\ref{sec:bmv}).
\item \emph{The stochastic remnant fails as the dark matter.} Bridge~A lies above 98\% of the SPARC data; a universal linear term fits
worse than the radial acceleration relation by $\Delta\mathrm{BIC}=+5066$; an accumulated term fits the rotation curves only as Milgrom's
interpolating function and is excluded in the solar system. The reading that survives is primordial black holes from the start as the
cold dark matter, which the rotation curves cannot tell apart from particle dark matter (Sections~\ref{sec:sparc}
and~\ref{sec:remnant}).
\item \emph{Everything else agrees, with three inherited tensions.} Of 33 scored confrontations, from MICROSCOPE and optical clocks to
DESI, 30 agree at 95\%, among them all 13 that test statements specific to GUID. The three that do not, evolving dark energy, the Hubble
constant and curvature, are tensions of $\Lambda$CDM; the scalar tilt of Starobinsky inflation, scored conditionally on that potential, is
a fourth. Rows that only restate the datum a prediction was taken from, or that overlap another row, are reported but not counted
(Section~\ref{sec:data}).
\item \emph{The Standard Model is tested where it is measured best}, against statements fixed in the repository before the analysis:
the Higgs couplings follow mass (slope $1.009\pm0.017$; holds), neutrinos are Dirac and neutrinoless double-beta decay does not occur
(live), and the proton-to-electron mass ratio varies with $\alpha$ at $-0.045$ times its rate, at least 400 times less than unification
predicts (live; the window we fixed beforehand was wrong by a factor of 4.5, a failed prediction we report). The gauge couplings never
meet within the Standard Model's running (Section~\ref{sec:sm}). %EXOTICS
\item \emph{Twenty-three statements of version~5.1 are corrected or sharpened}, from the 95\% bound on the information scalar to the
dark-matter verdict (Section~\ref{sec:corrections}).
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{What we claim.} We claim that the derivations are as written, that every number reproduces from public scripts, and that the
verdicts follow from the measurements cited. The scripts and their checks are: \texttt{docs/guid-v6/checks.py} (125 of 125) for Part~I;
\texttt{docs/qg-verdict/assembly.py} (18 of 18) for the holographic assembly; and in \texttt{docs/simulation/},
\texttt{hosted\_benchmarks.py} (53 of 53), \texttt{holographic\_code.py} (77 of 77), \texttt{gravity\_simulated.py} (110 of 110),
\texttt{bmv\_witness.py} (32 of 32), \texttt{sparc\_rotation.py} (36 of 36), \texttt{remnant\_history.py} (39 of 39) and
\texttt{predictions\_vs\_data.py}, whose 6 numerical checks pass and whose 33 scored confrontations are results, 30 of them consistent.
Appendix~\ref{app:repro} gives runtimes, seeds and data provenance. We do not claim that the account is correct, and we name in
Section~\ref{sec:break} where we expect it to break.

\paragraph{What is new and what is not.} Every physical prediction of this paper already belongs to an existing programme: classical
gravity coupled to quantum matter and its decoherence-diffusion trade-off~\cite{oppenheim,osswd}, gravity as a measurement-and-feedback
channel~\cite{ktm,tilloydiosi}, gravitationally mediated entanglement as the test~\cite{bose,mv,ggs}, horizon
thermodynamics~\cite{unruh,hawking75,jacobson95}, Christodoulou's mass formula~\cite{christodoulou}, holographic codes with a classical
centre~\cite{harlow}, primordial black holes as dark matter~\cite{gortongreen}. Fixed by its own bridge hypotheses, GUID's coupling lands
on the Di\'osi--Penrose rate or on a refuted value (Section~\ref{sec:bridges}), with the nuclear smearing length left to experiment. GUID is
therefore not a new theory of quantum gravity; it is a member of the classical-quantum family. What it adds is a route and four
statements: (i) a derivation of the classical side of the open question from an information principle, namely that a structure every
party can audit in full cannot be quantum (Lemma~\ref{lem:classical}), which is the redundancy principle of quantum
Darwinism~\cite{zurek,bph} imposed as an axiom, where the gravitational literature postulates classicality or leaves it to experiment;
(ii) the identification of DMP's record-and-recompute architecture with measurement-and-feedback gravity (Proposition~\ref{pr:ktm});
(iii) a rule, DMP's balance, that holds the decoherence-diffusion trade-off at equality, so that a measured gravitational decoherence would
fix the spacetime noise (Section~\ref{sec:bridges}); Section~\ref{sec:gravsim} shows that equality is exactly measurement and feedback
with a complete record, so the rule says that the frame adds no noise beyond its own record; (iv) the converse lesson for the design of
information systems, that a control structure every party can audit in full is classical while the data it governs need not be. All four
rest on Hypotheses~\ref{h:exist} to~\ref{h:info} and~1$'$, which are identifications, not derivations. Two results of this version hold
independently of GUID: under Di\'osi--Penrose decoherence the ratio of the entangling to the decoherence rate of any two spheres is
mass-independent and at most 1, so no entanglement test can then separate classical from quantum gravity (Section~\ref{sec:bmv}); and the
spin witness of the original proposal, as printed in its arXiv version, does not certify entanglement (the form quoted in later work does,
but only for entangling phases above $\pi/2$), while a two-setting witness adapted to the state detects every entangling phase, with
stated thresholds under decoherence (Section~\ref{sec:bmv}).

\subsection{How to read this paper}

Part~I states the proposal; Part~II simulates it; Part~III confronts it with data; Part~IV collects the predictions, the ledger of every
statement with its verdict, the corrections to version~5.1, and where we expect the account to break. Every step carries one of four
marks. \tagD{} follows from the text of DMP~0.9.9 (commit \texttt{4400cce} of its repository~\cite{dmp}). \tagH{} is a hypothesis of
GUID: an identification of a DMP word with a physical quantity, or a bridge principle, which no derivation supplies. The identifications
are stated in Sections~\ref{sec:fields} and~\ref{sec:cosmo}; the balance rule and the two bridges in Section~\ref{sec:bridges}; the reading
of ``full reach over the act'' as reach over existence in Section~\ref{sec:holo}. \tagP{} is established physics, with its source. \tagS{}
is a result of a simulation in this paper. Every statement that meets data receives one of eight verdicts: \emph{derived} (it follows from
the axioms and the hypotheses named), \emph{holds} (it agrees with measurement and follows from the steps given), \emph{holds as a
reading} (it agrees, but adds nothing a measurement could tell apart from the established account; a rederivation of a known result is
one), \emph{refuted} (a measurement or calculation contradicts it), \emph{untested} (no measurement yet reaches it), \emph{live} (it can be
tested and a test is possible or under way), \emph{open} (neither GUID nor physics yet answers it), or \emph{inherited} (a tension with data
that GUID shares unchanged with $\Lambda$CDM or with the inflation model it names). A label such as
\chk{Q5} names a check in \texttt{docs/guid-v6/checks.py}; \chk{A3} names check~3 of the holographic assembly; a label such as
\chk{gravsim:G2} or \chk{data:D3.4} names a result of a simulation script, listed in Appendix~\ref{app:repro}. Units are SI unless stated;
where we set $G=c=1$ or $\hbar=c=k_B=1$ we say so. The metric signature is $(-,+,+,+)$. Two symbols carry two meanings, as in the
literatures they come from, and the context fixes which: $\lambda$ is the coupling $\dd\ln\alpha/\dd\phi$ of the information scalar
(Sections~\ref{sec:fix},~\ref{sec:alpha} and~\ref{sec:data}) and the decoherence rate of a superposition (Sections~\ref{sec:bridges},
\ref{sec:gravsim} and~\ref{sec:bmv}); $\eta$ is the E\"otv\"os ratio of free fall and, with indices, the Minkowski metric.
