# Refutation: Team Fable against two claims about DMP

Team Fable, the adversary, 2026-10-27. The brief: disprove (A) "DMP is a sound, useful information-theoretic system" and (B) "DMP is a
useful approach to quantum gravity". Read: DMP 0.9.8 (`45d3822`: chapter 01, G0, T39, T40), gravity#19 (`docs/physics.md`, levels 0b to
0s), gravity#18, gravity#17 (`NoGo*.lean`, `Physics.lean`, `Stitch.lean`, F11 to F26), gravity#20, dmp#24. Every row names its target,
steelmans it, attacks it with a counterexample, a Lean theorem or a published result, and ends in one verdict. The Lean counter-models
are `evidence/Dmp/Refute.lean` (`rf_*`); the pinned toolchain could not be installed in the drafting session, so their kernel check is
this pull request's `lean-evidence` run, not a claim of this file. Theorem numbers are DMP 0.9.8's. Nothing here decides; the owner does.

## Claim B: a useful approach to quantum gravity

| Target | Steelman | Attack | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **B1** "Something exists" (level 0b: "needed, holds, picks the entangled branch") | A theory needs a non-empty domain. | In first-order logic ∃x (x = x) is a theorem, and no physical theory fails it, so it separates nothing. The "need" is misattributed: `NoGoAxioms.axioms_hold_without_gravity` exhibits a `World` with G = 0 and says nothing about an empty world. The branch is picked by "an existence space that is the hierarchy of information" read as spacetime from entanglement (van Raamsdonk 2010; Swingle 2012): a physical conjecture. | refuted: redundant as stated, a disguised premise as used |
| **B2** The causal-set reading (level 0k; "a minting from G0") | Order plus number is geometry (Malament 1977; Bombelli, Lee, Meyer and Sorkin 1987). | Axiom 3's order is a tree: the past of every line is a chain (`rf_past_is_a_chain`). A causal set embedded in Minkowski space of two or more dimensions has unrelated pairs in every past, and its fraction of related pairs is a constant of the dimension (Myrheim 1978; Meyer 1988); a tree's fraction falls to zero, which no dimension gives. Sequential growth lets an element be born above any set, not only below the first (Rideout and Sorkin 2000). Λ ~ 1/√N is Poisson counting plus unimodular gravity (Sorkin 1997; Ahmed, Dodelson, Greene and Sorkin 2004), neither in DMP, and its cosmologies are constrained (Barrow 2007; Zwane, Afshordi and Sorkin 2018). | refuted: false of DMP's order; the number is not DMP's |
| **B3a** "Holds" at level 2: Lovelock, Jacobson 1995 | Metric, conserved current, second order force Einstein. | All three inputs come from the dictionary (vector = tetrad, rate = lapse), none from DMP; `clausius_fails` and `axioms_do_not_fix_gravity` are gravity#17's own. The conserved current is false of DMP, whose information is destroyed on schedule (T27, T34 road 3). | analogy; refuted as derivation |
| **B3b** Entanglement equilibrium (level 0g: the maintained median state as Jacobson 2016) | Maximal vacuum entanglement gives the full equation. | Jacobson's hypothesis is stationarity of a *state* under a joint variation, with a UV-finite entanglement density and a conjecture for non-conformal matter (arXiv:1505.04753 v4); no rate, no scheduler. Level 0g names this refuter and leaves it to "a physicist". | analogy; undecided by #19's own admission |
| **B3c** Holographic codes (levels 0b, 0c: "any three of five" as G0's five) | Majority of existence has a theorem in quantum secret sharing. | The code's links are entanglement across cuts of an AdS tiling; DMP's are uncoupled (`dmp_field_uncoupled`) and locally verified, and level 0b concedes the branch "is no longer DMP". Five legs of a perfect tensor and T5's "more than half" share a number, which level 0m calls, of the constants, "two fives that happen to agree". | analogy |
| **B3d** The Planck tick (level 0s: Salecker and Wigner; Ng and van Dam; time as the longest chain) | Gravity bounds any clock; a sprinkled order carries its own time. | The bound is on a clock's resolution for a given mass and running time; DMP's tick is `R1Wave`'s root step, signed by both witnesses, a global synchronous clock: the preferred frame level 0s says a lattice must not have. "Longest chain" needs a Poisson sprinkling; through a tree it is the depth (`rf_past_is_a_chain`), no Lorentzian time in it. | analogy |
| **B3e** Information converted into energy at a border (level 0s: "derives" S ≤ 2πER/ħc from Landauer plus collapse) | Bekenstein argued the bound from a hole and the second law. | Landauer needs a temperature; DMP has none and no energy (gravity#17 §5). Inserting the Hawking temperature, which already carries the area law, gives E/(k T_H ln 2) = 8πGM²/(ħc ln 2) against the bound's 4πGM²/(ħc): the scaling is T_H's and the constant is off by 2/ln 2. Bekenstein 1981 and Casini 2008 derive the bound otherwise; level 7's own argument (T38 makes the interior extensive) stands. | refuted as derivation |
| **B4a** "Gravity is classical" (gravity#18 T-A to T-D; level 0h) | Two witnesses reading a record in full broadcast it; no-broadcasting forces commuting states. | No-broadcasting needs a non-commuting set to begin with; a record of verdicts (`Entry.ext`) is classical by construction, so T-C assumes its conclusion, and T-D turns "trust is gravitational" from metaphor into equation. The project's witnesses disagree: #18 picks classical, #19 (levels 0b, 0s) entangled; a theory that predicts both predicts neither. The experiment is live and disputed (Aziz and Howl 2025; Marletto, Oppenheim, Vedral and Wilson 2025; Diósi 2025; Gundhi, Infantino and Bassi 2026). | refuted (not implied) |
| **B4b** r = 0 (levels 0n to 0p: "no Existence derived to bend it") | Unquantised gravity has no primordial tensor modes (Krauss and Wilczek 2014). | r = 0 follows from an unquantised metric, not from DMP: Weyl = 0 describes the background, and tensor modes are fluctuations of the quantised metric at an amplitude set by H (Starobinsky 1979). #19 moves DMP to the entangled branch at 0s, where r > 0; the trans-Planckian bound it cites gives 10⁻³⁰, not zero, from quantised fluctuations (Bedroya, Brandenberger, Loverde and Vafa 2020); the oscillon peak belongs to the preheating papers it cites. | refuted (not implied) |
| **B5** The consistency relations of level 0r (r = −8 n_t; f_NL = (5/12)(1 − n_s)) as "tests of DMP compliance" | Potential-independent relations are the right test. | Both are theorems of single-field slow-roll inflation from an inflaton action (Liddle and Lyth 1992; Maldacena 2003); DMP supplies no premise. A sky that breaks either refutes single-field inflation and leaves DMP untouched; "more than one field, against 0p's first act" misreads single-field as single-act, since multi-field models also start every mode in one Bunch–Davies vacuum. | refuted as DMP's; survive as inflation's |
| **B6a** This thread: "information ... transitions into energy; the inverse is also true" | Landauer 1961 and the Szilard engine (Toyabe et al. 2010) trade bits for work. | Under unitary evolution S(ρ) and ⟨H⟩ are each conserved, so neither converts into the other; the trade runs through a bath at the bath's rate kT ln 2, the temperature's number, not the information's. A maximally mixed qubit has energy and no information; the vacuum has zero energy and an area's worth of entanglement entropy (Bombelli, Koul, Lee and Sorkin 1986). | refuted as an identity; survives as two inequalities with T and R in them |
| **B6b** "Entanglement is those instances where [vector and scalar] transition, every time information acts" | Interactions generically entangle (Zurek 2003); a scalar does decay into vector quanta. | Both directions fail. A CNOT on \|00⟩ returns \|00⟩: an interaction, no entanglement (Nielsen and Chuang 2000). Entanglement swapping entangles parts that never met (Żukowski, Zeilinger, Horne and Ekert 1993); the vacuum is entangled across every pair of regions with nothing acting (Reeh and Schlieder 1961). Fields do not transition: spin is a Casimir label (Wigner 1939), a scalar quantum becomes two vector quanta or none. Level 0h already ruled "a property of the state, not of events". | refuted |
| **B6c** "Entanglement is a function of DoE collapse into a lower layer" | A layer down in a MERA adds entanglement. | DMP's "lower" is the coarse-graining direction (level 0q, the hash), under which correlations cannot grow (data processing; LOCC monotonicity, Vedral and Plenio 1998), and a collapse is the zero of entanglement: after a projective measurement the measured part is pure, hence a product (a Bell pair measured on one side is \|00⟩ or \|11⟩). T37's collapse is an item no act reaches: on the entangled branch, a product state. | refuted (sign reversed) |
| **B6d** "... a universal private and public key whose hash results in the Planck constant" | A key pair is a duality of a public and a hidden half. | ħ is dimensionful: its value is a choice of units and no digest yields it (Duff, Okun and Veneziano 2002, level 0p's own citation); the constants do not vary between events (Rosenband et al. 2008; Uzan 2011). A hash is many-to-one, quantum evolution one-to-one (`dissolution_forgets`, gravity#17). A private key is private by a complexity assumption (Rivest, Shamir and Adleman 1978) that quantum dynamics itself breaks (Shor 1994). | refuted |
| **B6e** "A giant universal CA ... what does not [agree] IS not ... measured as entanglement" | Certificate chains are hierarchical trust with a root. | A certificate chain is public, copyable and verified by one party alone (RFC 5280); entanglement is unclonable (Wootters and Zurek 1982) and locally invisible (a half of a maximally entangled pair is maximally mixed). The effect of an inaccessible lower layer on this one is decoherence, not entanglement (Zurek 2003), and the interference of C60 bounds it (Arndt et al. 1999). "What is not certified is not" meets dark matter, certified by nothing but the existence half (Zwicky 1933; Rubin and Ford 1970). One universal CA is a single origin of trust, which T19 forbids and Axiom 1 excludes. | refuted |

## What survives, in three lines

1. **The Lean as a model of its own definitions:** a meet along a parent-array chain, pigeonhole, self-stabilisation, a non-injective digest; sound, kernel-checked, one witness, satisfied by the inert system.
2. **The engineering synthesis:** separation of privilege, zero-trust live verification, leases, audit counts, quorums, under one vocabulary; no new primitive, and the vocabulary ("gravity", "horizon", "field") never becomes an equation.
3. **Of the physics, the classical map only:** containment as a one-way boundary, no hair, the record as a growing area; every "holds" past it is a published result with DMP's words beside it, and every prediction is the published programme's, which the project's own two witnesses assign to opposite branches.
