## 1. In plain words

**The question.** The paper's release criterion asks for "Grand Unified": SU(3) x SU(2) x U(1) in one structure, with at
least one checkable consequence. The lead was: the Standard Model gauge group is believed to be
(SU(3) x SU(2) x U(1)) / Z_6 with Z_6 = Z_2 x Z_3, and that quotient is what puts electric charge in thirds. The owner's
readings put 2 and 3 at the base. Does the 2-then-3 reading give that quotient as a consequence?

**The answer, short.** **No, not as a consequence.** It gives a clean, exact *description*, and one *reading-selected
choice* whose checks are already known. In detail:

* **The structure is exact and now in Lean.** The centre elements of SU(3) x SU(2) x U(1) that act trivially on the
  Standard Model fields form exactly a cyclic group of order 6, generated by (omega, -1, e^{2 pi i/6}); it splits as an
  order-2 part on SU(2) x U(1) and an order-3 part on SU(3) x U(1) (the CRT split already proved for every SU(N)). Any
  particle invariant under it has electric charge Q with Q + (triality)/3 an integer: colour singlets have integer
  charge, quarks charge in -1/3 + Z. The block embedding into SU(5) has exactly this Z_6 as its kernel, so SU(5)
  contains the quotient group. All [Lean] (section 4); all of it is [classical].
* **What is input, not derived.** The hypercharges. The Z_6 comes out of them. Nothing in DMP or in the project's
  formal pieces produces the numbers 1/6, 2/3, -1/3, -1/2, -1, 1/2. Classically they are fixed (up to scale) by anomaly
  cancellation or by embedding in SU(5); that is the known route, not ours.
* **Why the 2-then-3 reading does not predict it.** (a) That the centre of SU(2) x SU(3) is Z_2 x Z_3 = Z_6 follows the
  moment one chooses the factors SU(2) and SU(3); the reading restates that choice, it does not make it. (b) Which part
  of that Z_6 is actually divided out (Z_1, Z_2, Z_3 or Z_6) is decided by the matter, and present experiments do not
  decide it (Tong 2017). (c) Z_6 = Z_2 x Z_3 is symmetric: it has no "first 2, then 3". The order appears only in the
  symmetry breaking (section 3.3), and there it is a reading.
* **The candidate.** The owner's entry "G0's U(1) possibility field" puts every SU(N) centre on one common phase
  circle. Read as "the centres of SU(2) and SU(3) are glued to the one U(1) as fully as the matter allows", it selects
  the maximal quotient Z_6 out of the four options. That choice is checkable: it forbids, forever, any colour-singlet
  particle with fractional charge, fixes the smallest magnetic monopole charge, and correlates axion couplings if an
  axion is found. But the same choice is the textbook expectation and is forced by any SU(5) or SO(10) unification. So
  it is a **falsifiable reading, not a new consequence**: it would count for the gate only if the selection itself came
  out of a DMP theorem stated before looking at the hypercharges, and that theorem does not exist.
* **Why the project's present pieces cannot see it.** All twist-sector results (`SUNTwist*`) are for pure gauge theory
  with one group. In pure gauge theory the whole centre is a symmetry; Z_6 is singled out only once matter is added. So
  the selection lives exactly where the project has nothing yet: matter, and in particular chiral fermions.

**Verdict for the gate.** "One structure": met in the modest, classical sense (the group S(U(3) x U(2)) inside SU(5),
kernel in Lean). "At least one checkable consequence, DMP-native": **not met**; this round finds no route from the
present formal pieces to a consequence that is both checkable and not already the standard expectation. Section 6 lists
what would change that.

## 2. The classical facts

### 2.1 The fields and the Z_6 [classical; Lean in section 4]

Convention Q = T_3 + Y. One generation and the Higgs, written as (SU(3), SU(2))_Y and with q = 6Y:

| field | rep | Y | q = 6Y | triality t | duality d | 2t + 3d + q |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q_L | (3, 2) | 1/6 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 6 |
| u_R | (3, 1) | 2/3 | 4 | 1 | 0 | 6 |
| d_R | (3, 1) | -1/3 | -2 | 1 | 0 | 0 |
| L_L | (1, 2) | -1/2 | -3 | 0 | 1 | 0 |
| e_R | (1, 1) | -1 | -6 | 0 | 0 | -6 |
| H | (1, 2) | 1/2 | 3 | 0 | 1 | 6 |
| gluons, W, B | adjoints | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

A centre element (u 1_3, v 1_2, z) with u^3 = 1, v^2 = 1, |z| = 1 acts on a field by u^t v^d z^q. It is trivial on all
fields exactly when (u, v, z) = (z^2, z^3, z) with z^6 = 1 (`sm_kernel`). The generator xi = (omega, -1, e^{i pi/3}),
omega = e^{2 pi i/3}, acts by e^{2 pi i (2t + 3d + q)/6}, and the last column is always a multiple of 6.

**Charge quantisation.** For a component with 2T_3 = s (s = +-1 in a doublet, 0 in a singlet), 6Q = 3s + q. If
6 | 2t + 3d + q then 6 | 6Q + 2t, i.e. **Q + t/3 is an integer** (`charge_thirds`) [classical]. So electric charge mod 1
is fixed by colour: 0 for colour singlets, 2/3 for triplets. This is the correlation of charge with colour that the Z_6
quotient expresses.

**Why the hypercharges are what they are** [classical]. Given the Standard Model representations of one generation,
cancellation of the gauge anomalies, together with the mixed gauge-gravitational anomaly, fixes the hypercharge ratios
(Geng and Marshak 1989; Minahan, Ramond and Warner 1990); with a right-handed neutrino a B - L direction opens unless a
Majorana mass is assumed (Babu and Mohapatra 1989). Alternatively the ratios follow from SU(5). Either way they are an
input to the Z_6, not an output of it.

### 2.2 The four global forms [classical]

Because every Standard Model field is invariant under the full Z_6, the gauge group can be
G_Gamma = (SU(3) x SU(2) x U(1)) / Gamma for any subgroup Gamma of Z_6: Gamma = Z_1, Z_2, Z_3 or Z_6. All four have the
same local physics and the same Feynman rules; present experiments do not decide between them (Tong, "Line operators in
the Standard Model", JHEP 07 (2017) 104, arXiv:1705.01853). Calling Z_6 "the" Standard Model group "puts the cart before
the horse" in Tong's phrase: it is the maximal choice the known matter allows, not one that is measured.

What changes with Gamma is which *new* particles are allowed (elementary arithmetic with the generators of section 4):

| Gamma | trivial on a field iff | colour singlet with Q = 1/3 | colour singlet with Q = 1/2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Z_1 | always | allowed | allowed |
| Z_2, generated by (1, -1, -1) | q = d mod 2 | allowed | forbidden |
| Z_3, generated by (omega^2, 1, omega) | 2t + q = 0 mod 3 | forbidden | allowed |
| Z_6 | 2t + 3d + q = 0 mod 6 | forbidden | forbidden |

Gamma also changes the allowed line operators (Wilson, 't Hooft and dyonic lines), hence the possible magnetic charges
of monopoles, and the periodicities of the theta angles (Tong 2017). In the language of generalised symmetries
(Gaiotto, Kapustin, Seiberg and Willett 2015; Aharony, Seiberg and Tachikawa 2013) the Z_6 that acts trivially on all
matter is an electric 1-form symmetry of the G_{Z_1} theory, and dividing by Gamma gauges the part Gamma of it. Recent
work ties heavy-particle spectra and 1-form symmetries to the faithful group (Li and Xu 2024, arXiv:2404.04229).

### 2.3 SU(5) and SO(10) [classical; the SU(5) kernel in Lean]

* **SU(5)** (Georgi and Glashow 1974). The block map (A, B, z) -> diag(z^{-2} A, z^3 B) sends SU(3) x SU(2) x U(1) onto
  S(U(3) x U(2)) inside SU(5); its kernel is {(z^2 1, z^3 1, z) : z^6 = 1}, the same Z_6 (`su5_embed_kernel`,
  `su5_embed_det`). So **any SU(5) unification forces Gamma = Z_6**. The 5 carries q = -2 on the triplet and q = 3 on the
  doublet; one generation fits in 5-bar + 10. Charge quantisation is automatic, since the charge is a traceless generator
  of SU(5).
* **SO(10)** (Georgi 1975; Fritzsch and Minkowski 1975) contains SU(5) x U(1) (up to a finite quotient) and the
  Pati-Salam group SU(4) x SU(2) x SU(2); one generation plus a right-handed neutrino fits in the 16. It contains SU(5),
  so it also forces Z_6. E6 contains SO(10).

### 2.4 What is checkable

| consequence | what decides it | status |
|---|---|---|
| charge-colour correlation (no colour-singlet fractional charge) | follows from Gamma = Z_6, hence from any SU(5)/SO(10) | all observed particles obey it; searches for free fractional charges in bulk matter and at accelerators found none (review: Perl, Lee and Loomba, Annu. Rev. Nucl. Part. Sci. 59 (2009) 47) [external] |
| proton decay | GUT gauge bosons (X, Y) | not seen; Super-Kamiokande tau/B(p -> e+ pi0) > 1.6 x 10^34 years at 90% CL (arXiv:1610.03597; enlarged-volume analysis arXiv:2010.16098). Minimal non-supersymmetric SU(5) predicted far shorter lifetimes and is excluded [external] |
| coupling unification, sin^2 theta_W = 3/8 at the unification scale | running of the three couplings | the three Standard Model couplings do not meet at one point; with supersymmetric running they nearly do (Amaldi, de Boer and Fuerstenau 1991) [external] |
| magnetic monopoles | 't Hooft-Polyakov (1974) in any GUT; the minimal Dirac charge depends on Gamma (Tong 2017) | not seen (MACRO, IceCube and others set flux limits) [external] |
| axion couplings | Gamma: for Z_6 the axion-photon coupling's fractional part is fixed by the axion-gluon coupling (Reece, JHEP 10 (2023) 116, arXiv:2309.03939; Choi, Forslund, Lam and Shao, arXiv:2309.03937, for q = 1, 2, 3, 6) | testable if a QCD axion is found [classical] |

So the "Z_6 versus the other quotients" question is checkable in principle, by three routes (a fractional charge, a
monopole's charge, an axion's couplings), but no present measurement settles it.

## 3. The DMP-native candidate

### 3.1 What the project's formal pieces can state [Lean, classical]

* **SU(N) centres as phases on one U(1).** Every centre element of every SU(N) is a scalar omega 1 with omega on the
  unit circle (`General/Centre.lean`). For the Standard Model this becomes: the centre of SU(3) x SU(2) x U(1) is
  (cube roots) x (square roots) x (circle), and the Z_6 is the diagonal copy of the sixth roots inside it
  (`sm_kernel_cyclic`) [Lean].
* **The CRT split.** `centre6 : ZMod 6 = ZMod 2 x ZMod 3` (`SUNTwistPrimes`) and, at the group level here, the order-2
  part (1, -1, -1) and the order-3 part (omega^2, 1, omega) (`sm_kernel_two`, `sm_kernel_three`) [Lean].
* **Twist sectors as 1-form-symmetry sectors.** The project's 't Hooft twists by omega^k (`SUNTwistWilson`) are exactly
  the centre (electric 1-form symmetry) sectors of pure SU(N) [classical]; the strict bound 0 < Z_tw,k < Z says each such
  sector is neither empty nor everything [Lean, per SU(N)]. The general chain (`SUNTwist.lean`) needs only a compact
  group and a twist family with inverses, so a product family k -> (omega^k, (-1)^k, e^{2 pi i k/6}) on a lattice
  SU(3) x SU(2) x U(1) pure gauge theory fits its hypotheses [reading of the hypotheses; not formalised].

### 3.2 Would 2-then-3 predict Z_6? No, and here is exactly why

| step | status |
|---|---|
| centre of SU(2) x SU(3) is Z_2 x Z_3 = Z_6 | [Lean] (CRT), but only *after* choosing SU(2) and SU(3) |
| why SU(2) and SU(3) (and not SU(5), or SU(2) twice) | **input** (observation); the readings "2 first, then 3" restate it |
| the hypercharge ratios | **input** (observation; classically also from anomaly cancellation or SU(5)) |
| given the hypercharges, the trivially acting subgroup is exactly Z_6 | [Lean] |
| given the hypercharges, charge in thirds correlated with colour | [Lean] |
| that the gauge group is the full quotient by Z_6 (not Z_1, Z_2, Z_3) | **not decided** by present experiment [classical]; forced by SU(5)/SO(10) [Lean for SU(5)] |
| that the 2-then-3 reading selects Z_6 | [reading] (section 3.3) |
| that any DMP theorem selects Z_6 | **[open]**; none exists |

In pure gauge theory, the project's present scope, the full centre Z_3 x Z_2 (and the U(1)'s own 1-form symmetry) is
unbroken by any matter; Z_6 is singled out only by the matter's charges. So the project's twist-sector theorems, which
are all pure gauge, cannot distinguish Z_6 from the other options. That is the precise reason this round finds no
consequence.

### 3.3 The reading, kept apart [reading]

* **"G0's U(1) possibility field" selects the maximal quotient.** If every centre phase is a point on the one shared U(1)
  circle, the natural gluing identifies the SU(2) and SU(3) centres with hypercharge phases as fully as the matter
  allows: Gamma = Z_6. The rival forms Z_1, Z_2, Z_3 keep part of the centres separate from the U(1). This is the
  sharpest DMP-flavoured statement found, and it is checkable through section 2.4's three routes. It is not a
  derivation: "as fully as the matter allows" uses the hypercharges, and the conclusion coincides with what SU(5) gives.
* **"2 first, then 3, with the hand-over at the state 2, 3."** The Z_6 has no order. An order does appear in the
  symmetry breaking [classical]: at the electroweak scale (about 246 GeV) the Higgs vacuum, the SU(2) "handshake" of the
  PREP entry, leaves the unbroken group (SU(3) x U(1)_em) / Z_3: the Z_2 part is absorbed, the Z_3 part survives to low
  energies as the colour-charge correlation of section 2.1, and SU(3) confines at a lower scale (a few hundred MeV).
  Reading: in descending energy the 2-part acts first and the 3-part remains: "2 then 3". This matches the owner's
  ordering; it predicts nothing beyond the standard picture.
* **SU(2) the stable profile (`BLOWUP.md` 7.3).** SU(2) = S^3 is the only stable one among the bi-invariant SU(N) under
  Ricci flow. That is a statement about the group manifold, not about the global form of the Standard Model group; no
  link to the Z_6 was found.
* **"SU(5) with a single coupling is G0 at GRH" (PREP naming entry).** SU(5) is the smallest simple group containing
  the Z_6 quotient (section 2.3, kernel in Lean). Its single coupling holds at one scale only, minimal SU(5) is excluded
  by proton decay, and the couplings do not meet exactly (section 2.4).

## 6. What would make a consequence checkable and DMP-native

Ranked by how directly they would answer the gate; none is promised.

1. **Derive the selection, not the arithmetic.** A DMP principle, stated before the hypercharges and proved as a theorem
   about running existence spaces, whose consequence is "the centre twists that survive matter are glued to the U(1)
   maximally". With the representation content as input it would output Gamma = Z_6, and hence the three checks of
   section 2.4. This is the only route found on which DMP would contribute a consequence rather than a description.
2. **Anomaly cancellation in Lean** (cheap, classical): from the one-generation representation content, the anomaly
   equations fix the hypercharge ratios, hence Z_6 (composing with `gen_trivial_iff`). Useful as the honest "where the
   hypercharges come from" step; it makes the input a classical theorem, not a DMP one.
3. **Twist sectors of the product group with Z_6-invariant matter.** Extend the `SUNTwist` chain to a lattice
   SU(3) x SU(2) x U(1) theory with vector-like matter (reflection positive, Osterwalder and Seiler 1978): the Z_6 twist
   sectors would be strictly between nothing and everything, while twists outside Z_6 would not even be defined. This
   would show *in the project's own language* that matter cuts the centre down to Z_6. Chiral matter, which the
   Standard Model needs, is a known hard problem on the lattice (Nielsen-Ninomiya) and stays out of reach.
4. **The single coupling.** The owner's "SU(5) with a single coupling": a statement about running couplings needs the
   continuum theory with matter, beyond Yang-Mills as treated here.

Until one of these lands, the paper can say exactly: the framework contains SU(3) x SU(2) x U(1) / Z_6 as one structure
(classical, Lean-stated); the owner's readings select Z_6, which is falsifiable by a fractional-charge discovery, a
monopole's charge or an axion's couplings; no consequence is derived from DMP.
